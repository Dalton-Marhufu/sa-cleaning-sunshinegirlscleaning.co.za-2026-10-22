## Score: 6/10

## Top 3 Issues
- Dated, cluttered Elementor layout with weak visual hierarchy — the logo, testimonials and pricing table compete for attention and no single value proposition leads.
- Weak conversion flow: the only real CTA is a soft "Get a quote" that scrolls to a form; no click-to-call, no WhatsApp, no sticky contact anywhere near the hero.
- Strong proof points (30+ years, 702/Sage Small Business Awards finalist, named testimonials, team photos, large job gallery) are under-used and scattered low on the page instead of front-and-centre.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Lead with the existing tagline "Your one stop, no nonsense cleaning company" as a bold hero headline, backed by click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons and a real hero photo.
- Turn the seven real service names into an image-led service grid, each card showing an actual job photo from their site.
- Surface trust up top — awards, years of experience, owner-supervised promise — plus a proper work gallery and prominent testimonials to convert visitors.
